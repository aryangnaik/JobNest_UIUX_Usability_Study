📌 Executive Summary & Final Conclusion Report

1. Project Overview & Objective
This study presents a comparative mixed-methods usability evaluation between the original Naukri.com job portal and JobNest, a redesigned high-fidelity prototype. The primary objective was to validate whether eliminating information clutter, adding upfront salary transparency, providing 1-click applications, and introducing real-time application tracking resolves candidate cognitive fatigue and application anxiety.

2. Qualitative Findings ("Think-Aloud" Protocol)
Across 5 core candidate scenarios, test participants vocalized significant friction on Naukri.com versus JobNest:
* Search & Filters: On Naukri, users reported feeling overwhelmed by ad banners, sponsored listings, and hidden pay scales ("Salary Not Disclosed"). On JobNest, the 3-input hero widget (`Role`, `Locality`, `Work arrangement`) enabled instant filter execution with 100% upfront salary visibility.
* Employer Authenticity: Naukri's dense, unverified consultancy posts created fear of recruitment spam. JobNest’s prominent `Verified employer` badges and clean typography restored candidate trust.
* Application Flow: Naukri's external ATS redirects (e.g., Workday) caused severe application fatigue due to duplicate form filling. JobNest's 1-click `Easy Apply` modal completed submissions in under 5 seconds.
* Status Tracking: Candidates felt stuck in an "ATS Black Hole" on Naukri due to static "Submitted" labels. JobNest’s visual status tracker (`Applied` → `Resume Viewed` → `Shortlisted` → `Interview Scheduled`) provided total transparency.
* Decision Making: Naukri lacked comparison tools, forcing multi-tab navigation. JobNest’s side-by-side comparison drawer allowed effortless evaluation across salary, work mode, and skill fit.

3. Quantitative & Survey Synthesis
Average Usability Score: Naukri.com received an average rating of 6.0 / 10, whereas JobNest achieved 9.1 / 10.
Maze Testing Benchmarks: JobNest achieved a **Direct Success Rate of 92%, a low Misclick Rate of 3.5%, and an overall Maze Usability Score of 92/100 (Grade A) across remote sessions.

4. Final Conclusion
The qualitative and quantitative data conclusively prove that the **JobNest modified prototype successfully improved the candidate user experience**, eliminating major friction points present on Naukri.com.
